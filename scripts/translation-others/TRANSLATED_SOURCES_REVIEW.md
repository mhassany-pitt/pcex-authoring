## 1. Reviewer Instructions

### How to Access and Review Sources in the PCEX Tool
1. **Direct PCEX Editor Link**:
   - Click on any **Spanish Editor Link** in the tables below (`https://adapt2.sis.pitt.edu/pcex-authoring/#/editor/<source_id>`).
   - The editor will open with all Spanish texts: Title, Description, Code lines, Comments/Explanations, and Distractors.
2. **Comparison with Original English**:
   - In the top right corner of the PCEX editor, click **"View Untranslated / Original"** (or open the English Editor Link side-by-side).
   - All original English texts (`untr_name`, `untr_description`, `untr_code`, `untr_content` comments, and `untr_*` distractors) are preserved in the same file.
3. **Key Review Points**:
   - [ ] **Title & Description**: Ensure natural phrasing and accurate technical terminology in Latin American Spanish.
   - [ ] **Code Integrity**: Variable, function, and class names are preserved; user-facing prompts in `input(...)` and literals in `print(...)` are translated.
   - [ ] **Line Comments & Explanations**: Verify pedagogical explanations are clear and accurate.
   - [ ] **Distractors**: Verify distractors, code fragments, and error explanations are properly translated.
4. **Approval & Tagging**:
   - All newly translated items currently carry the tags:
     - `spanish-latam;color=yellow`
     - `llm-translation;color=blue`
     - `gpt5mini;color=purple`
     - `validation-pending;color=orange`
   - Once reviewed and approved, replace `validation-pending;color=orange` with `validation-approved;color=green` (or remove the pending tag).
